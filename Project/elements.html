BTS

Bangtan Sonyeondan

BTS is a greatest K-pop boy group ever. They have achieved tremendous success both in South Korea and internationally. BTS debuted on June 13, 2013. They debuted under Big Hit Entertainment.

The group's Korean name, 방탄소년단 (Bangtan Sonyeondan), can be translated as “Bulletproof Boy Scouts.”
