A General Conference talk about the importance of the temple: I Love to See the Temple

Video about Laie Temple:


The Laie Temple is a special place to me. Whenever I see the temple, I feel peaceful and reminded of God’s love for me. The temple is more than just a beautiful building. It is a place where I can slow down, pray, and feel closer to Heavenly Father.


One of the greatest blessings of the temple is the peace I feel when I am there. Life can sometimes be stressful and confusing, but the temple helps me remember what is truly important. It gives me strength to keep going and reminds me that I am not alone.


I am grateful to have the Laie Temple close to me. I hope I can continue to make the temple a part of my life and carry the peace I feel there with me wherever I go. I hope that as I continue to visit the temple, I can become more patient, faithful, and Christlike in my everyday life. I want the feelings I have in the temple to stay with me even after I leave, and I hope I can share that same peace and love with the people around me.
