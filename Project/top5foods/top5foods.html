Why I chose this topic

I chose this topic because I love food and enjoy trying different dishes. Food is not just something we eat every day, but it also brings happiness and good memories. I want to share my top 5 favorite foods and explain why they are special to me.

Bun Bo

Bun Bo is a Vietnamese noodle soup with beef, pork, and various herbs.

Pho

Pho is a traditional Vietnamese noodle soup with beef or chicken broth, rice noodles, herbs, and meat.

Goi Cuon

Goi Cuon is a Vietnamese fresh spring roll with shrimp, pork, herbs, and rice paper.

Hu Tieu

Hu Tieu is a Vietnamese noodle soup with pork, shrimp, and various herbs.

Steak

Steak is a popular Western dish with grilled or pan-seared beef.
