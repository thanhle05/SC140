About BTS

BTS is a South Korean boy group that debuted in 2013 with seven members: RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. They started as a small group with big dreams and worked hard to become one of the most famous music groups in the world. Their music is not only about love and happiness but also about struggles, mental health, and learning to accept yourself. Through their songs, BTS encourages people to follow their dreams and never give up, even when life gets difficult.

The Message Behind Their Music

One of the things I admire most about BTS is how they encourage people to love themselves and be confident in who they are. Their leader, RM, often shares meaningful messages about life, happiness, and not worrying too much about what other people think. BTS reminds me that everyone has their own journey, and we should not compare ourselves to others. Their music teaches me that it is okay to make mistakes, face challenges, and grow at my own pace.

"Life is fun, and living is better." — RM of BTS
"Your life isn't yours if you always care what others think." — RM of BTS
